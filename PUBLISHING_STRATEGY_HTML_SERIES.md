# Publishing Strategy: HTML Series Launch Plan

**Project:** The Great Inversion + Companion Essays (HTML Publishing)

**Prepared For:** /home/luism/projects/the-great-inversion

**Date:** May 18, 2026

**Primary Assets Covered:**

- `index.html` (main article)
- `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html`
- `companion-unreliable-agent.html`
- `burning-question.html`
- Future HTML release of `burning-question.html` (working name: `burning-question-v2.html`)

---

## 1) Objective

Publish the main essay and companion HTML articles as a coherent series that:

- positions the core thesis first (`index.html`),
- deepens authority through focused companion pieces,
- maximizes cross-page readership through intentional internal linking,
- creates a clear launch path for the upcoming revised Burning Question HTML edition.

Success criteria (first 30 days):

- Strong completion/read-depth on `index.html` and at least one companion per reader cohort.
- Companion traffic sourced from internal article links and social distribution, not only direct links.
- Clean analytics attribution per post and per platform.

---

## 2) Publication Architecture

## Core Narrative Stack

1. `index.html` (pillar):
   The central thesis and framing for the full series.

2. `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html` (historical depth):
   Expands historical legitimacy and long-arc framing.

3. `companion-unreliable-agent.html` (risk and governance):
   Operational realism for engineering and leadership audiences.

4. `burning-question.html` (economics and infrastructure):
   The macroeconomic and systems constraint lens.

5. `burning-question-v2.html` (future release):
   Update cycle artifact with revised data, clearer scenarios, and stronger decision framing.

## Internal Link Graph (required)

Every published page should link to:

- the main article (`index.html`),
- at least two other companions,
- a consistent “Read Next” block at the end.

Recommended “Read Next” order:

- From `index.html` -> all 3 companions.
- From `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html` -> `index.html`, `companion-unreliable-agent.html`, `burning-question.html`.
- From `companion-unreliable-agent.html` -> `index.html`, `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html`, `burning-question.html`.
- From `burning-question.html` -> `index.html`, `companion-unreliable-agent.html`, `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html`.

When `burning-question-v2.html` is live:

- Add a top-page note in `burning-question.html`: “Updated edition available”.
- Add reciprocal links between v1 and v2.

## 3) Rollout Sequence

## Phase 0: Preflight (T-3 to T-1 days)

Checklist:

- Validate all HTML pages load correctly on desktop/mobile.
- Confirm meta title, meta description, social preview image tags, and canonical tags.
- Verify internal links and anchor links.
- Confirm each page has a visible byline/date and clear relation to the series.
- Prepare UTM-tagged URLs per platform.

## Phase 1: Core Launch (Day 1)

Publish/promote in this order:

1. `index.html` (morning)
2. `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html` (later same day, as “extended chapter”)

Rationale:

- Establish the thesis before distributing specialized arguments.
- Use the historical companion to increase authority and dwell time.

## Phase 2: Reinforcement (Day 3-5)

Publish/promote:

1. `companion-unreliable-agent.html`
2. `burning-question.html`

Rationale:

- Follow conceptual framing with practical and economic implications.

## Phase 3: Refresh Window (Week 3-6)

Publish:

- `burning-question-v2.html` (future revised HTML edition)

Rationale:

- Re-engage prior readers.
- Demonstrate evolving evidence and disciplined updates.
- Generate a second peak without rewriting the full series.

## 4) Channel Strategy

## LinkedIn (primary)

Audience:

- Engineering leaders, CTOs, principal engineers, founders.

Cadence:

- 2 posts/week during launch cycle.

Format:

- Data-led opening, 3-5 tight insights, one explicit CTA.

Post map:

- Post A: `index.html` thesis statement.
- Post B: `companion-fifty-years-paradigm-shifts.html` historical arc.
- Post C: `companion-unreliable-agent.html` guardrails vs guarantees.
- Post D: `burning-question.html` AI economics and energy.
- Post E (future): `burning-question-v2.html` “what changed since first release”.

## X / Twitter (secondary)

Cadence:

- 4-6 short posts per week during first two weeks.

Format:

- One sharp claim + one data point + one link.
- Use threads for companions only when referencing 2+ sources.

## Facebook / broader audience channels (optional)

Cadence:

- 1 post/week.

Format:

- More explanatory language; fewer technical assumptions.

## 6) Tracking and Analytics

## URL Convention

Use one canonical URL per page and campaign-tagged distribution links:

- `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=great_inversion_series`
- `?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=great_inversion_series`
- `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=great_inversion_series`

For future release:

- `?utm_campaign=burning_question_v2_launch`

## Event Tracking (minimum)

Track:

- Page view per asset.
- Scroll depth milestones (25/50/75/90).
- Outbound clicks from each “Read Next” block.
- Time-on-page buckets (e.g., 30s, 90s, 180s, 360s).

KPIs:

- Companion CTR from `index.html`.
- Return visitors who consume more than one page.
- Comparative completion: `burning-question.html` vs `burning-question-v2.html`.

## 7) Editorial QA Checklist

Before each publish or re-publish:

- Title and subtitle are consistent with positioning.
- Claims that rely on data have source references in-page.
- “Back to Main Article” and “Read Next” links are functional.
- Social preview title/description are not truncated.
- Mobile typography and spacing remain readable.
- No broken anchors or missing asset references.

For `burning-question-v2.html` additionally:

- Add a “What changed since v1” section near top.
- Keep v1 available for transparent comparison.
- Explicitly date-stamp revisions.

## 8) Future Burning Question Release Plan (v2)

## Why ship a second HTML edition

Use v2 to:

- integrate new evidence,
- tighten scenario framing,
- respond to reader objections,
- create a fresh distribution moment without changing the series backbone.

## Suggested v2 structure

1. Executive update (what changed since v1).
2. Revised data table (old vs new indicators).
3. Scenario updates (bull/base/bear with trigger signals).
4. Implications for engineering leaders and platform buyers.
5. Appendix: methodology and source update notes.

## Launch script for v2

- T-2 days: Teaser post (“updated numbers + revised scenarios”).
- Launch day: Publish `burning-question-v2.html` + update internal links from all pages.
- Day +2: Publish comparison thread (“3 assumptions that changed”).
